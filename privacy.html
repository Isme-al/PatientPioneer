Patient Pioneer ("we," "us," "our") provides clinical trial recruitment services to research sites and sponsors. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you visit our website or engage our services.

01 — Scope of this policy

This policy applies to information collected through:

  • Our website and any forms submitted through it
  • Email correspondence with our team
  • Service engagements with research sites and sponsors
  • Patient-facing recruitment campaigns we operate on behalf of clients

It does not apply to data held by third-party platforms (study sponsors, EMR systems, CTMS platforms) outside our direct control.

02 — Information we collect

From research sites and sponsors

  • Contact details (name, role, email, phone, organization)
  • Study information (protocol number, indication, phase, enrollment targets, I/E criteria)
  • Operational details necessary for engagement scoping and delivery

From prospective trial participants

  • Contact details (name, email, phone, ZIP code)
  • Health-screening responses provided voluntarily through our pre-screening forms
  • Demographic information relevant to study eligibility

Automatically collected

  • Standard web analytics (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referral source)
  • Device and session metadata necessary for site operation

03 — How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: matching prospective participants to study eligibility and routing referrals to research sites
  • Communication: responding to inquiries, sending engagement updates, and coordinating with research site coordinators
  • Service improvement: analyzing recruitment funnel performance to refine outreach strategy
  • Legal compliance: meeting obligations under applicable healthcare, advertising, and data-protection laws

04 — Sharing & disclosure

We share information only as necessary to deliver our services or as required by law:

  • With research sites: pre-screened participant referrals are shared with the contracting research site for the specific study they consented to be considered for
  • With service providers: hosting, email, CRM, and analytics vendors operating under written confidentiality and data-protection agreements
  • For legal reasons: when required by subpoena, court order, regulatory request, or to protect rights, safety, and property

We do not sell personal information. We do not share participant health-screening responses with anyone other than the research site they consented to be referred to.

05 — HIPAA & Protected Health Information

Important context Patient Pioneer is generally not a HIPAA-covered entity. However, when engaged by a research site that is a covered entity, we operate as a Business Associate under a written Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and handle Protected Health Information (PHI) accordingly.

When acting as a Business Associate:

  • PHI is handled only for the purpose specified in the BAA
  • Access is limited to personnel with a documented need to know
  • PHI is transmitted and stored using encrypted, access-controlled systems
  • Breach notification protocols are followed per 45 CFR § 164.410

06 — Data security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect information, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest for sensitive data stores, role-based access controls, and routine review of vendor security posture. No system is fully secure; we do not guarantee absolute security.

07 — Data retention

  • Inquiry contact data: retained for up to 24 months from last contact, unless an active engagement begins
  • Engagement records: retained for the duration of the engagement plus 7 years for tax, audit, and regulatory purposes
  • Participant pre-screening data: retained per the contracting site's protocol and regulatory requirements; deleted on participant request unless retention is legally required

08 — Your rights

Depending on where you reside, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent to recruitment outreach

To exercise these rights, email hello@patientpioneer.com. We respond within 30 days.

09 — Cookies & analytics

We use minimal first-party analytics to understand site performance. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies through your browser settings without losing access to core site functionality.

10 — Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11 — Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve or as legal requirements change. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after changes indicates acceptance.

12 — Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns:

  • Email: hello@patientpioneer.com
  • Subject line: "Privacy inquiry"

We respond to privacy inquiries within 5 business days and to formal data-rights requests within 30 days.
